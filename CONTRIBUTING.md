# Contributing

[docs/DEVELOPMENT.md](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md) says how Pley is built and
tested, and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) the rules every change keeps: the
checks to run, licensing, security, and how commits read.

## The licence of a contribution

Pley is under the GNU AGPL, version 3 ([LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)), and
its authors can also license it on other terms. So that both stay
possible, what you contribute comes under the
[Apache License, version 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0),
which lets it be part of Pley under either.

The Kodi add-on (`apps/kodi`) is the exception: contributions to it come
under its own licence, the GPL version 2 or later.

Opening a pull request confirms that you wrote what it contains, or have
the right to contribute it on these terms.
